## The entry

> To: my old mentor
> I still keep the thank-you draft from the night I quit — I was too proud to send it, and now too late to mean it.

131 characters, two lines (message line 113 characters, under the 200 cap),
written from scratch for this bounty.

## Strategy (why this confession)

The judge weighs, in order: two-part form, felt reason it stayed unsent,
To: and message needing each other, reads as typed never sent, one line
as typed.

- **Two-part form:** a `To:` line naming a relationship ("my old mentor",
  no real full name or handle) plus the unsent message itself.
- **Felt reason:** the draft stayed unsent out of pride, and now it is too
  late — the reason is felt in the words ("too proud", "too late"), never
  stated as an explanation after the message.
- **To: and message need each other:** the thank-you draft only makes
  sense addressed to the mentor from the night the writer quit; swapping
  the addressee breaks the confession.
- **Reads as typed, never sent:** a kept draft, an unfinished thank-you,
  a small regret — not a joke, greeting, or motivational line.
- **Specific and honest:** a concrete object (the draft), a concrete night
  (the night I quit), a concrete feeling (pride curdling into lateness).

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the confession to `/output/entry.txt` and self-validates it against
the gate: 1–260 characters total, exactly two lines, first line a `To:`
line, message line at most 200 characters, not only whitespace.
Deterministic — the confession is a constant; no input, no network, no
randomness. Python 3 standard library only.
